Our Story

金华宝脉生物科技有限公司 · ProbioMine Biotechnology Co., Ltd. — an innovation-driven biotech company at the intersection of microbiome science, single-cell technology, and artificial intelligence.

Who We Are

Revolutionizing Health with Precision Microbiome Science

At ProbioMine Biotech, we are revolutionizing health with personalized probiotics and precision microbiome analytics. Using advanced metagenomics and Raman-activated single-cell sorting (RACS) technology, we deliver tailored microbial solutions designed around each individual's unique biology — combining innovation, personalization, quality, and ethics in everything we do.

Founded in 2026 and based in the Pan'an Precision Medicine Industrial Park in Jinhua, Zhejiang, the company was established under a national young-talent recruitment program. With a standardized laboratory base of over 700 m², we provide high-precision microbiome analysis for difficult clinical samples and mine functional strains for the next generation of probiotic products.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become the world's leading platform for low-biomass microbiome analytics and functional strain discovery.

Mission

To lower the technical barrier of microbiome research through technological innovation, empower the full scientific workflow with AI agents, accelerate the clinical translation of microbiome science, and advance precision medicine.
