Technology Platform

Four integrated platforms turn difficult, low-biomass clinical samples into precise microbial readouts — and turn mixed communities into validated, functional probiotic strains.

The Problem

Why a New Approach?

The Gut Microbiome Matters

The gut microbiome is a complex community of trillions of microorganisms that aid digestion, produce vitamins, and protect against pathogens. Imbalances caused by poor diet, stress, or antibiotics lead to health issues — and microbes in oral, skin, tumor and blood sites carry major clinical significance.

Low-Biomass Samples Break Conventional Tools

Samples from oral, skin, tumor tissue or blood contain extremely low microbial biomass and suffer severe host-DNA contamination. Conventional 16S rRNA and metagenomic sequencing fail to capture reliable microbial signals, leaving valuable samples unanalyzable.

Probiotics Need Personalization

Commercial probiotics show highly variable responses across individuals and often fail to colonize the gut. The traditional "culture-first, screen-next" strategy is slow and labor-intensive. Personalized probiotics ensure a better match with the user's gut environment and stronger colonization.

Platform 01

2bRAD-M — Low-Biomass Microbiome Profiling

2bRAD-M (Type IIB Restriction-site Associated DNA for Microbiome) is our proprietary reduced-metagenomics platform, purpose-built for low-biomass clinical samples. By sequencing tags at specific restriction sites across microbial genomes, it achieves high-precision species identification where conventional methods fail.

Technical Advantages

  • Ultra-high precision — species-level accuracy of at least 92%
  • 91.1% cancer-typing accuracy on clinical FFPE samples
  • Works on oral, eye, blood, skin, tissue, breast milk, feces, urine, FFPE and household-environment samples
  • QC pass rate improved by over 50% versus traditional methods on complex samples
  • Validated by 90+ academic publications within three years

Platform 02

Raman-Activated Single-Cell Sorting & Functional Culturomics

Combining FlowRACS single-cell Raman screening with a culturomics platform (DCP), we screen functional strains directly in situ from samples — no sequencing, no plating — while keeping cells alive for downstream culture and validation.

Screen-First, Culture-Next

  • High-throughput, function-targeted screening in situ
  • FlowRACS screens high-yield mutant libraries at 120 cells/min
  • First single-cell viability and vitality profiling of mixed probiotic products
  • Raman flow cytometry (RFC) processes 1 cell per second for rapid high-throughput analysis
  • Technical principles: Raman spectroscopy, stable-isotope labeling, microfluidics
  • Mines personalized probiotics for autologous transplantation
