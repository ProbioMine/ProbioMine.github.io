For Individuals

The Personalized Probiotic Plan

Autologous probiotics enhance colonization and integration because they are tailored to the individual's own gut environment — designed around your health needs and targeted conditions.

Fecal Sample Collection

A stool sample is collected from the individual.

Raman-Based Sorting

Probiotic strains are sorted from your own gut microbes by Raman-activated single-cell sorting.

Process & Store

Selected strains are processed and stored for later reintroduction.

Colonization Confirmation

Follow-up analysis confirms colonization and integration of the formulation.

Effective relief from constipation. An autologous probiotic formulated from each individual's unique gut microbiota to treat constipation.
