Core Platforms

From Difficult Samples to Actionable Biology

Four tightly integrated technology platforms cover the full chain — detection, analysis, mining, and validation — closing the loop from low-biomass clinical samples to functional strains.

🧬

2bRAD-M Microbiome Profiling

Reduced metagenomic sequencing purpose-built for low-biomass and host-contaminated samples — oral, tumor tissue, FFPE, blood, skin, breast milk, urine and more — with species-level precision.

Learn more
🔬

Single-Cell Raman Sorting

Raman-activated cell sorting (FlowRACS) coupled with culturomics (DCP) mines functional strains in situ — screen-first, culture-next — keeping cells alive for downstream validation.

Learn more
📈

MiX Early-Warning Models

AI models built on microbiome and multi-omics data quantify chronic-disease risk across oral, gut and skin health — from halitosis and caries to periodontal and gut indices.

Learn more
🤖

AI-Agent Analysis Platform

Autonomous agents automate the entire bioinformatics workflow — from raw sequencing data to analysis reports, meta-analysis, and scientific writing — accelerating discovery end to end.

Personalized Probiotics

Autologous Probiotics, Mined From Your Own Gut

Commercial probiotics show highly variable responses and often fail to colonize. We take a different route: isolate the right strains from your own gut microbes, formulate them, and reintroduce them — designed for your unique health needs.

Sample Collection

Fecal sample collection from the individual.

Raman-Based Sorting

Raman-activated sorting of probiotic strains from your own gut microbes.

Process & Store

Strains are processed and stored for later reintroduction.

Colonization Confirmation

Follow-up profiling confirms engraftment and integration.
